## 1. Executive Summary & Brand Positioning

### The Mission
ONTON is the premier event operating system built natively inside Telegram. By merging frictionless Web2 ease-of-use with Web3 ownership, ONTON allows organizers to launch events, collect free RSVPs in one tap, accept Telegram Stars and crypto payments, gate official Telegram groups automatically, and issue verified Proof-of-Attendance badges at the door.

### The Problem We Solve
| Competitor / Approach | Attendee Experience | Organizer Pain Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Luma / Eventbrite** | Must leave Telegram, enter email, manually search for chat links in spam folder | High drop-off; organizers manually manage Telegram member approvals; fragmented data. |
| **Traditional Web3 Event DApps** | Forced wallet connection, network switching, and gas fees just to RSVP for a free event | 80%+ drop-off from non-crypto natives; slow and confusing. |
| **ONTON v2.0 (The Telegram Event OS)** | **1-tap Free RSVP** directly in Telegram. Optional Telegram Stars (Apple/Google Pay) or TON/USDT. Instant private group link via Bot DM. | **0% friction.** Automated chat gating, door QR scanner, and verifiable Proof-of-Attendance SBTs. |

### Core Value Pillars
1. **1-Tap Free RSVP (Zero-Friction):** No crypto wallet or gas fee required to register for free events.
2. **Dual-Rail Payments:** Buy tickets in Telegram Stars (`XTR`) via Apple Pay / Google Pay, or in TON / USDT Jettons via TonConnect.
3. **Automated Telegram Group Gating:** Confirmed attendees automatically receive a private, single-use invite link via Telegram Bot DM.
4. **Door QR Scanner & Proof of Attendance:** Fast venue check-in automatically unlocks digital Soulbound Token (SBT) badges.
