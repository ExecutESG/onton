### Refund & Cancellation Safeguards:
- **Client Cancellation:** If a client cancels with >72 hours notice, a 10% administrative fee is deducted and 90% is refunded. If canceled <48 hours, advance payments made to channels are non-refundable.
- **Partner Failure:** If a channel fails to host an AMA or fails SLA quality checks, the channel payout is forfeited, and the client is given a replacement session in another Tier-1 channel at zero extra cost.
