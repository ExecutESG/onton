Historical Context · 1 Year Ago

What Held ONTON Back in 2024/2025

100% Crypto-Only Friction

Every attendee was forced to connect TonConnect and pay gas fees in TON/USDT. No Telegram Stars existed. Non-crypto users dropped off immediately.

Permission-Gated Launching

Event creation required manual whitelisting, hardcoded UUIDs, or admin approvals. Normal community leaders couldn't self-serve launch.

Gamification Noise

The product was cluttered with Play2Win tournaments, Genesis Onion campaigns, and task farming. It confused serious event hosts.

The Takeaway: Onton had real tech, but the user experience was fighting against its own adoption.
The Strategic Pivot

Positioning as "The Luma of Telegram"

The Market Opportunity
  • 950M Telegram Users: Events are discussed on Telegram, but organizers currently send attendees to external Web2 links (Luma, Eventbrite).
  • Luma Lacks Telegram Native Power: Luma cannot check Telegram IDs, auto-add to private event chats, or accept native Telegram Stars.
  • Web3 Ownership Without Web3 Friction: Anyone can RSVP in 1 tap; Web3 badges are minted automatically in the background.
What We Did
  • Killed the noise: Officially retired Play2Win, task-farming, and lottery gimmicks.
  • 100% Permissionless: Anyone can spin up an event in 60 seconds with custom slugs.
  • Dual-Rail Checkout: Telegram Stars (in-app micro-payments) + TonConnect crypto.
