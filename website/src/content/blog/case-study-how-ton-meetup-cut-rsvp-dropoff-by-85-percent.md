## Deep Dive: The Operational Friction of Web2 Event Forms

The Helsinki Web3 Developer Community hosts monthly meetups, hackathons, and technical workshops for developers across Northern Europe. Historically, their event operations relied on a disjointed combination of Google Forms, Luma web pages, and manual Telegram community management.

### The Breakdown of the Old Funnel:
1. **The Invitation:** An event announcement was posted in the community's primary Telegram group (1,800 developers).
2. **The Redirection:** Clicking the RSVP link kicked developers out of Telegram into Safari or Chrome.
3. **The Friction:** Users had to enter their email, verify an email confirmation code, connect a desktop browser wallet extension, and answer multiple survey questions.
4. **The Chat Gating Nightmare:** Once registered, organizers sent a static Telegram group link via email. Within 2 hours, that link was leaked into public crypto groups, flooding the developer chat with hundreds of spam bots and unverified users.

Community managers spent **18 hours before every event** manually verifying wallet addresses, kicking uninvited accounts from Telegram chats, and managing door registration spreadsheets.

---

## The Solution: Deploying ONTON's Telegram-Native Event OS

In Autumn 2024, the organizers migrated 100% of their operations to **ONTON (`@theontonbot`)**:

- **Native [Telegram Mini App (TMA)](/resources/glossary/tma-telegram-mini-app) Checkout:** Clicking the announcement link opened the RSVP sheet immediately inside Telegram. Pre-authenticated Telegram profile data eliminated 90% of form typing.
- **Frictionless Payments with [Telegram Stars](/resources/glossary/telegram-stars):** For workshops requiring ticket fees, attendees paid with Apple Pay in a single tap without needing prior crypto balances.
- **Automated [Token Gating](/resources/glossary/token-gating):** The ONTON bot automatically generated a secure, single-use Telegram chat invite link. No links leaked, and zero unauthorized users entered.
- **Dynamic [QR Code Door Check-In](/resources/glossary/qr-code-door-checkin):** Door staff scanned attendees' rotating QR codes using the organizer scanner bot, minting a [Soulbound Token (SBT)](/resources/glossary/soulbound-token) upon entry.

The result was an **85% reduction in registration abandonment**, zero manual administrative overhead, and an authentic, high-signal developer networking environment.
