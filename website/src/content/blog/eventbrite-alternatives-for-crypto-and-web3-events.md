## 2. Luma (lu.ma)

**Best For:** Silicon Valley tech dinners and general startup gatherings.

Luma has become popular in tech circles due to its clean aesthetic and calendar invites. However, for Web3 organizers, significant friction remains:
- **High Fees:** Combined platform and Stripe fees reach 7.9% + 30c per ticket.
- **Email Heavy:** Relies on email confirmations, whereas crypto users frequently ignore email in favor of Telegram notifications.
- **Limited Web3 Identity:** Lacks deep integration with on-chain identity standards and native Telegram bot workflows.

## 3. Moongate

**Best For:** Traditional EVM NFT ticket ticketing campaigns.

Moongate provides NFT ticketing software with support for Ethereum and Polygon chains.
- **Strengths:** Strong utility for token-gating based on legacy Ethereum NFT collections.
- **Weaknesses:** High gas friction for non-technical attendees; requires users to connect desktop MetaMask extensions rather than mobile Telegram wallets.
