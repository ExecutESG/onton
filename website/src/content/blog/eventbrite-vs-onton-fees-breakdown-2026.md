## Detailed Fee Breakdown: Eventbrite vs. ONTON

### 1. Platform Service Fees
- **Eventbrite:** Charges **3.7% + $1.79 per ticket** on paid tiers. In addition, Eventbrite requires monthly organizer subscription fees for events exceeding 25 attendees.
- **ONTON:** Charges **0.0%** for free RSVPs and crypto payments (TON/USDT), and a modest **1.5% to 2.0%** for native [telegram-stars](/resources/glossary/telegram-stars) purchases. There are zero monthly recurring subscription fees.

### 2. Payment Processing Fees
- **Eventbrite:** Charges standard credit card merchant processing of **2.9%** on the gross transaction amount.
- **ONTON:** When attendees purchase with crypto via [ton-connect](/resources/glossary/ton-connect), network gas is fractions of a cent on TON, and payment processing is **0%**. For users buying with Apple Pay or Google Pay via Stars, settlement is automated.

### 3. Payout Timelines & Cash Flow Traps
- **Eventbrite:** Typically holds attendee funds until **up to 5 business days after the event has successfully concluded**. If you need ticket revenue upfront to pay venue deposits or caterers, you are forced to apply for restricted merchant advances.
- **ONTON:** Organizers receive crypto funds directly into their own non-custodial TON wallet in real-time as tickets are purchased. Funds are never escrowed or locked by a third party.
