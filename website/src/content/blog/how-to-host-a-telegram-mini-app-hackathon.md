> **Why Telegram Mini Apps?**
> With Telegram exceeding 950 million monthly active users, building Web3 consumer apps as [Telegram Mini Apps (TMAs)](/resources/glossary/tma-telegram-mini-app) has become the fastest route to viral distribution. Organizing a hackathon inside Telegram connects protocols directly with world-class builders.

## 1. Setting Up Track Bounties & Prize Pools

A successful hackathon requires clear, actionable developer tracks. We recommend organizing bounties into four high-demand categories:

1. **Consumer & Social Mini Apps:** Viral games, referral mechanics, and social graph tools using Telegram APIs.
2. **DeFi & Payment Flows:** Seamless checkout integrations using [telegram-stars](/resources/glossary/telegram-stars) and [ton-connect](/resources/glossary/ton-connect).
3. **Decentralized Identity & Privacy:** SBT credentials, Proof-of-Humanity filters, and verifiable credentials.
4. **Tooling & Infrastructure:** Developer SDKs, indexing bots, and automated deployment pipelines.

---

## 2. Managing Submissions and Anti-Plagiarism on Telegram

Managing hundreds of GitHub repositories and project demos across disparate Google Forms or Discord channels leads to chaos.

### The ONTON Hackathon Workflow
1. **Builder Registration:** Developers join via the ONTON Mini App and receive a "Hacker SBT" verifying their participation.
2. **Team Formation Chat Gating:** Builders are automatically invited into [token-gated](/resources/glossary/token-gating) mentor channels and track discussion groups.
3. **Demo Submission:** Project repos and video demo links are logged directly via the bot.
4. **On-Chain Judging:** Mentors and judges vote using Soulbound voting credentials to prevent Sybil manipulation.

## 3. Real-Time Prize Payouts & SBT Diplomas

Traditional hackathons often take 60 to 90 days to distribute cash prizes through complex wire transfers.

With ONTON:
- Bounties in TON or USDT are distributed directly to winning builders' non-custodial wallets via batch transactions.
- All finalists receive a permanent, non-transferable [proof-of-attendance](/resources/glossary/proof-of-attendance) [soulbound-token](/resources/glossary/soulbound-token) verifying their hackathon achievement on their on-chain resume.

## Best Practices for Hackathon Track Design & Sponsor Alignment

To attract serious senior engineers and product designers, a hackathon must align bounties with the latest technological developments in the Telegram ecosystem:

### 1. Telegram Stars Commercial Applications
Challenge teams to build consumer Mini Apps that accept [Telegram Stars](/resources/glossary/telegram-stars) and cash out via [Fragment](/resources/glossary/fragment-exchange). Encourage projects in ticketing, AI agents, localized digital goods, and peer-to-peer creator subscriptions.

### 2. TON Blockchain Smart Contracts & Jettons
Incentivize developers to build decentralized applications utilizing [Jettons](/resources/glossary/jetton) and [TON Connect](/resources/glossary/ton-connect) for seamless decentralized finance and social coordination.

### 3. On-Chain Identity & Event Infrastructure
Reward builders designing decentralized attendance systems, [Event Badge SBT](/resources/glossary/event-badge-sbt) utilities, and [Token Gating](/resources/glossary/token-gating) bots that automate community management across Telegram channels.
