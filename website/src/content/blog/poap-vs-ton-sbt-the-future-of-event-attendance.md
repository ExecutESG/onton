## The Gas Crisis and the Shift from Ethereum to TON

When the [Proof of Attendance Protocol (POAP)](/resources/glossary/proof-of-attendance) launched in 2020 on Ethereum mainnet and later migrated to Gnosis Chain, it proved a critical premise: people cherish verifiable proof of their historical presence at developer gatherings, hackathons, and community summits.

However, as Web3 matured and expanded into mobile-first communities on Telegram, the legacy POAP infrastructure created insurmountable user hurdles:
1. **The External Wallet Requirement:** Attendees arriving at a conference check-in desk were forced to download standalone mobile apps or install MetaMask/Rainbow wallets to claim their badges.
2. **Claim Link Vulnerabilities:** Organizers typically distributed POAP badges via QR codes that pointed to static claim URLs. Malicious actors frequently took photos of the QR codes from across the room or forwarded the links into public Discord channels, allowing remote airdrop farmers to drain the entire badge supply before attendees could claim them.
3. **Speculative Secondary Markets:** Because standard POAP badges are ERC-721 tokens, they can be freely transferred and sold on secondary marketplaces like OpenSea. A user who never stepped foot inside Devcon could simply purchase an attendee badge, completely degrading the authenticity of on-chain participation metrics.
