## The Conversion Bottleneck in Web3 Events

Organizers hosting crypto conferences, side events, and exclusive hacker houses have long faced a brutal dilemma:
1. **Traditional Ticketing Platforms (Eventbrite, Ticketmaster):** Charge exorbitant fees (up to 10% including payment processing) and cannot natively verify on-chain wallet credentials or issue cryptographic credentials.
2. **First-Generation Web3 Platforms (Luma, Tokenproof):** Suffer from severe registration drop-off. Over **42% of prospective attendees abandon checkout** when prompted to install or connect an external browser extension wallet on mobile.

**Telegram Stars changes this calculus permanently.** With over 950 million active users already inside Telegram, native in-app payments provide the conversion power of Web2 e-commerce combined with the programmable ownership of Web3.

## What are Telegram Stars?

Launched by Telegram as an official in-app virtual currency, [telegram-stars](/resources/glossary/telegram-stars) allow users to acquire digital goods and services across [Telegram Mini Apps (TMAs)](/resources/glossary/tma-telegram-mini-app). Users purchase Stars directly through Apple In-App Purchases (iOS), Google Play Billing (Android), or on desktop using credit cards and crypto via [Fragment](/resources/glossary/fragment-exchange).

For organizers, Stars represent the ultimate checkout experience:
- **Instant 1-Tap Purchase:** Pre-authorized native payment sheet.
- **Global Liquidity:** Available to users worldwide without banking restrictions.
- **Direct Payouts:** Developers and organizers can cash out earned Stars into TON cryptocurrency via Fragment or reinvest them in Telegram Ads.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can attendees purchase tickets without owning cryptocurrency?
Yes! That is the primary superpower of Telegram Stars. Attendees can purchase Stars directly using Apple Pay, Google Play, or local credit cards inside Telegram.

### How do event organizers withdraw their funds?
Organizers can withdraw their accumulated Telegram Stars into TON cryptocurrency via the Fragment platform, or receive direct TON/USDT payments into their connected TON wallet.

### What happens if an attendee refunds a ticket?
When an organizer approves a refund, ONTON automatically revokes the attendee's ticket QR code, burns or invalidates the attendance SBT, and removes the user from the private event Telegram group.
