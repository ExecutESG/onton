When selling tickets for digital summits, Web3 conferences, or paid networking masterminds, choosing the right payment processor directly dictates your bottom line and conversion rate.

For years, Stripe was the default payment infrastructure. However, the introduction of [telegram-stars](/resources/glossary/telegram-stars)—Telegram's official virtual currency integrated directly with iOS In-App Purchase, Google Play Billing, and the [Fragment](/resources/glossary/fragment-exchange) platform—has created a compelling alternative.

In this analysis, we compare Telegram Stars against Stripe across **effective fees, checkout conversion, chargeback liability, and international liquidity**.

## 1. Checkout Conversion: The Mobile Friction Test

The average mobile cart abandonment rate for credit card checkout forms exceeds **68%**. When an attendee is required to type a 16-digit card number, CVV code, billing address, and wait for a 3D Secure SMS OTP on their mobile phone, the friction leads to lost sales.

With Telegram Stars:
- Attendees authorize payment using FaceID or fingerprint recognition through Apple Pay or Google Play.
- Zero form filling is required.
- The transaction settles inside the Telegram client in under 400 milliseconds.
- Organizers report a **25% to 40% higher conversion rate** on mobile-driven traffic.

---

## 2. Chargeback and Fraud Protection

Chargebacks represent a massive risk for high-ticket conference passes ($500 to $2,500). Unscrupulous attendees frequently attend an event and subsequently initiate a "friendly fraud" chargeback with their credit card issuer claiming unauthorized purchase.

- **With Stripe:** The burden of proof lies entirely on the merchant. If you lose the dispute, Stripe confiscates the ticket revenue and assesses a **$15.00 dispute penalty**.
- **With Telegram Stars & TON:** Transactions are cryptographically signed on-chain or authorized through Telegram's platform infrastructure. Chargeback fraud is completely eliminated, protecting your event's treasury.

---

## 3. Global Reach & Financial Inclusion

Stripe is only available to registered businesses in 46 countries. Organizers and attendees in Latin America, Southeast Asia, Eastern Europe, and Africa face severe restrictions or outright account rejections.

Telegram Stars bypasses banking borders entirely:
- Any user with a smartphone can acquire Stars directly inside Telegram.
- Organizers worldwide can withdraw earned Stars into TON cryptocurrency via [Fragment](/resources/glossary/fragment-exchange) or convert them directly into Telegram Ads credit to grow their event channel.

---

## When to Use Which?

* **Choose Telegram Stars if:** Your audience is active on Telegram, mobile conversion is critical, you host international attendees, and you want automated chat gating and [proof-of-attendance](/resources/glossary/proof-of-attendance) badges.
* **Choose Stripe if:** You cater strictly to traditional corporate enterprise buyers who require corporate American Express card processing with multi-tiered procurement approval workflows.
