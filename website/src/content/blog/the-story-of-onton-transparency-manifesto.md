## Part 1: The Spark — Building the Luma of Web3 (Summer 2024)

In June 2024, we started ONTON with a clear, singular realization:
**Event ticketing was completely broken for the 900+ million users of Telegram.**

Event organizers hosting crypto meetups in Dubai, Singapore, and Berlin were forcing attendees to leave Telegram, open mobile browsers, deal with popup ad-blockers, connect desktop extension wallets, and then manually request access to private Telegram chats. Over **40% of prospective guests dropped off before finishing registration**.

We launched `@theontonbot` to fix this forever:
- **1-Tap RSVPs:** Direct native [Telegram Mini App](/resources/glossary/tma-telegram-mini-app) experience inside Telegram.
- **TON Wallet Connect:** Seamless on-chain verification without leaving the chat.
- **[Soulbound Tokens (SBTs)](/resources/glossary/soulbound-token):** Verifiable digital passport badges proving attendance.

The response was immediate and explosive:
- By July 2024, over **75,000 users** had registered for 150+ community events.
- By mid-September 2024, we passed **100,000 monthly active users**.
- A week later, we doubled to **200,000 monthly active users**.
- We were featured in the official **Telegram Apps Center** (`@tapps_bot`) and co-hosted meetups with the **TON Foundation**, **TON Society**, and ecosystem leaders across Helsinki, Singapore, and Bali.

## Part 2: The Security Incident & Radical Accountability (November 2024)

Growth masked underlying technical debt. On **November 18, 2024**, a persistent scanner discovered an unauthenticated endpoint in our API that leaked guest list metadata for a specific private event.

In Web3, many teams conceal breaches. We chose transparency. On December 29, 2024, we released an open public disclosure ([Post #154](https://t.me/ontonlive/154)), shut down the endpoint, audited our permissions model, and guided the ethical reporter to the **TON Society HackenProof bug bounty program**.

That crucible taught us our first hard engineering rule: **never sacrifice cryptographic security for speed.**

---

## Part 3: The Gamification Trap & Genesis ONIONs (Spring 2025)

By early 2025, the broader TON ecosystem was swept up by "tap-to-earn" games and points farming. We made a strategic error: **we let the hype dictate our roadmap.**

Instead of doubling down exclusively on enterprise event ticketing software, we introduced:
- **ONTON Points & Quests:** Daily tasks and referral rewards.
- **The Genesis ONIONs NFT Collection:** 50,000 total supply (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum merge) launched with TON Society via a "Spin-to-Reserve" mechanic.

Thousands of community members spent their TON to spin for rare NFTs, expecting immediate token airdrops and life-changing returns. On **May 30, 2025**, with over 10,000 views on our channel, we took the official **Genesis Season Snapshot**.

We had built immense community expectation. But we were about to hit a wall that no code could prevent.

---

## Part 4: The War & The Emergency TGE Freeze (June 20, 2025)

Our Token Generation Event (TGE) was scheduled for June 2025. The claim interface went live on June 10. Then, the real world intervened.

On **June 20, 2025**, we broadcast an emergency announcement ([Post #419](https://t.me/ontonlive/419), 12.6k views):
> *"Due to the unforeseen and escalating conflict between Iran and Israel — and given that the ONTON core team is currently based in Iran — we must delay today's scheduled Token Generation Event (TGE)... this temporary delay is necessary to ensure that all key contributors, including our founder and core team members, can participate safely and effectively."*

Airstrikes, emergency sirens, and nationwide internet blackouts shut down our operations. As founders, we were managing team physical safety while trying to protect smart contract deployment keys.

Six days later, as connectivity partially returned, we resumed operations. Rather than seeking predatory private venture capital, we launched the **ONION Fair Launch Auction on TBook** throughout July 2025 to put the tokens directly in community hands.

---

## Part 5: The DEX Listing, Casual Mini-Games, and the Silence

On **August 22, 2025**, following a live video AMA on **Binance Live**, we officially launched our token under the ticker **$ONIT** on the **STON.fi DEX** (`EQCYFlnasDS18rfSF_CLRT0W5DJOYNbZKWJzaCK4gwVzlZPc`).

Eight days later, on August 30, 2025 ([Post #512](https://t.me/ontonlive/512)), **we permanently revoked all contract admin rights**, capping the supply at exactly **1,000,000,000 $ONIT**. No one — not even our team — could ever inflate the supply.

### But then we lost our way.
Faced with challenging crypto market conditions and low DEX liquidity, we attempted to maintain community engagement by launching casual Telegram mini-games: **2048**, **Math Battle**, and **Color Memory** tournaments for 500 $ONIT prizes.

It was the wrong move. Event organizers didn't care about 2048; they needed an enterprise event operating system. Meanwhile, $ONIT staking pools on JVault offered APY without external software revenue to sustain it.

On **October 21, 2025 ([Post #525](https://t.me/ontonlive/525))**, we announced a partnership with ZYNC. 

**And then... the channel went dark.**

For nearly a year, `@ontonlive` stood silent. We didn't exit scam. We didn't dump tokens. But our silence hurt our community just as much. Early NFT holders who had supported us felt stranded. The ruins of the past were all that remained.

## Part 6: The Return to First Principles

During the quiet months of 2026, we relocated, restructured our engineering foundation, and went back to the drawing board. We realized two fundamental truths:

1. **The Problem is Bigger Than Ever:** In 2026, Web3 events are larger than ever (Token2049, Devcon, ETHDenver, KBW), but legacy ticketing platforms like Eventbrite and Luma still charge up to 8% fees and suffer from massive wallet-connect friction.
2. **Telegram Stars Solved the Web3 Onboarding Problem:** With [telegram-stars](/resources/glossary/telegram-stars), attendees can pay in 1 tap using Apple Pay, Google Pay, or local cards without ever needing a crypto wallet extension.

**We are not building a meme coin. We are building the indispensable event operating system for Telegram and Web3.**
