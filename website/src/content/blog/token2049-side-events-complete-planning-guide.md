## 1. Timeline & Milestone Planning (8 Weeks Out)

To ensure your side event isn't competing for scraps the week of the conference, follow this battle-tested countdown:

* **8 Weeks Out:** Lock your event concept, format (VIP dinner, rooftop mixer, or builder workshop), and preliminary sponsor target list.
* **6 Weeks Out:** Secure and pay deposit on the venue. In Dubai (DIFC, Palm Jumeirah, Downtown) and Singapore (Marina Bay, Tanjong Pagar), prime venues sell out 45 days in advance.
* **4 Weeks Out:** Launch event page on **ONTON (`@theontonbot`)** and submit to the **[Token2049 Side Events Directory](/events/token2049-dubai-2026)**.
* **2 Weeks Out:** Activate automated Telegram group chat gating so confirmed attendees can begin networking before landing in the city.
* **Event Day:** Deploy door bouncers with the ONTON QR scanner for 1-second check-ins and [proof-of-attendance](/resources/glossary/proof-of-attendance) [soulbound-token](/resources/glossary/soulbound-token) issuance.

## High-ROI Venue Strategies for Dubai and Singapore

Choosing the right venue sets the tone for your brand during major conference weeks:

### 1. The Rooftop Sunset Mixer (Dubai Marina / Downtown)
- **Vibe:** High-energy networking, sponsored cocktails, panoramic views of the Burj Khalifa or Dubai Marina.
- **Target Crowd:** 150 to 350 founders, venture capitalists, and ecosystem executives.
- **Ticketing Tip:** Issue curated tickets with [Telegram Stars](/resources/glossary/telegram-stars) to eliminate scalpers, and distribute a custom [Event Badge SBT](/resources/glossary/event-badge-sbt) upon arrival.

### 2. The Private Founder & Whale Dinner (DIFC / Marina Bay Sands)
- **Vibe:** Intimate 25-person seated three-course dinner.
- **Target Crowd:** Core protocol founders, tier-1 venture fund partners, and key market makers.
- **Ticketing Tip:** Use ONTON's curated approval queue and automated [Token Gating](/resources/glossary/token-gating) to keep the guest list strictly confidential.

### 3. The Hacker Villa / BUIDL Hub (Palm Jumeirah / Sentosa)
- **Vibe:** Multi-day co-working villa with high-speed internet, private pool, catering, and evening lightning demo sessions.
- **Target Crowd:** 40 to 60 vetted protocol developers and smart contract engineers.
- **Ticketing Tip:** Deploy [Dynamic QR Door Check-In](/resources/glossary/qr-code-door-checkin) so participants can move in and out of the venue securely without wristbands.
