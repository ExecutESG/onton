Promoting a Web3 conference or side event requires a completely different playbook than traditional B2B marketing. Traditional email newsletters and LinkedIn ads yield abysmal conversion rates in crypto. Web3 builders, investors, and founders live in two primary ecosystems: **Telegram** and **Twitter/X**.

To sell out your conference, you must leverage native Telegram distribution, viral referral incentives, co-host networks, and on-chain status credentials.

In this playbook, we break down the exact week-by-week marketing blueprint used by top crypto conferences and side event organizers.

---

## Phase 1: 8 Weeks Out — The Early Bird Stealth Drop

The biggest mistake organizers make is announcing ticket sales to an empty room. Build anticipation before public launch:

1. **Whitelisting Strategic Communities:** Partner with top Telegram alpha groups, developer DAOs, and protocol foundations. Offer exclusive early-bird access gated by a [soulbound-token](/resources/glossary/soulbound-token) or special bot invite link.
2. **Co-Host Stacking:** Secure 3 to 5 recognized ecosystem partners as co-hosts. Each co-host agrees to blast their Telegram announcement channel on launch day.
3. **The Scarcity Anchor:** Release only 15% of total capacity in Tier 1. Announce a strict 72-hour window or 100-ticket cap to trigger FOMO.

---

## Phase 2: 4 Weeks Out — Native Telegram Amplification

Telegram is where ticket purchasing decisions happen in real time. Minimize attendee drop-off by removing web browser redirects.

* **In-App Checkout via Mini Apps:** Send prospective attendees directly into `@theontonbot`. Because the user is already authenticated inside Telegram, they can RSVP or pay via [telegram-stars](/resources/glossary/telegram-stars) in 1 tap without filling out lengthy registration forms.
* **Automated Private Networking Chat:** Immediately drop ticket buyers into a private, [token-gated](/resources/glossary/token-gating) attendee group. This creates early networking momentum, encouraging attendees to invite their colleagues.
* **On-Chain Speaker Announcements:** Announce keynote speakers and panel hosts with dynamic social cards showing their verified speaker SBT credentials.

---

## Phase 3: 2 Weeks Out — Viral Referral & Incentive Loops

Turn your registered attendees into your most effective sales force using game mechanics:

### 1. Dual-Sided Telegram Referral Bonuses
Give every registered attendee a personalized referral link generated by ONTON. When 3 friends register through their link:
- The referrer receives an automatic VIP lounge upgrade or exclusive invite to the private speaker dinner.
- The referred friend receives a 15% ticket discount.

### 2. Proof of Attendance Collectibles
Tease the exclusive commemorative [proof-of-attendance](/resources/glossary/proof-of-attendance) badge that attendees will receive upon physical door check-in. In Web3, on-chain attendance badges act as verifiable career credentials.

---

## Phase 4: 72 Hours Out — Overcoming the Dreaded Drop-Off

Free RSVP events in Web3 typically suffer from a catastrophic 50% to 70% no-show rate. Organizers often order catering and rent venues for 500 people, only for 180 to attend.

Here is how ONTON eliminates no-shows:
1. **Automated Telegram Direct Reminders:** Push automated reminders 48 hours, 24 hours, and 2 hours before kickoff directly through `@theontonbot`.
2. **Micro-Commitment Deposits:** Charge a 50 Stars ($1.00) refundable attendance deposit. If the attendee scans their [qr-code-door-checkin](/resources/glossary/qr-code-door-checkin) at the door, their deposit is instantly refunded or converted into drink tokens.
3. **Real-Time Venue Capacity Alerts:** Broadcast live capacity updates to your waitlist when tickets are claimed.
