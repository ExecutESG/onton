## The Landscape of Web3 Event Management

Over the last 4 years, Luma has established itself as the default RSVP tool for tech startups. However, crypto event organizers running side events at Token2049, Devcon, ETHDenver, and KBW frequently encounter critical bottlenecks:

1. **Attendee Fragmentation:** Event RSVPs happen on Luma via web browsers, but event networking and community announcements happen exclusively on Telegram. Organizers must manually copy links or pay for third-party Zapier webhooks to sync guest lists into Telegram groups.
2. **High Platform Rake:** Luma charges 5% plus standard Stripe card processing fees (2.9% + 30¢), totaling nearly **8% per paid ticket**.
3. **Sybil & Bot RSVPs:** Free Luma events are notoriously overwhelmed by bot registrations and airdrop farmers using temporary email addresses.
