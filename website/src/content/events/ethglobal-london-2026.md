## About ETHGlobal London 2026

**ETHGlobal London 2026** is Europe's premier Ethereum hackathon, bringing together over 2,000 Web3 developers, smart contract engineers, protocol architects, and venture partners for 36 hours of competitive hacking and ecosystem building.

Surrounding the central hackathon, London's vibrant tech corridor across Shoreditch and Canary Wharf hosts dozens of high-intensity side events, protocol pitch competitions, and developer workshops.

---

## Featured Side Event Categories

- **L2 Scaling & Interoperability Summits:** Deep technical workshops focusing on EVM rollups and cross-chain messaging.
- **DeFi & Programmable Liquidity Hacker Houses:** Private team incubators for algorithmic lending and AMM protocols.
- **Telegram & Account Abstraction Demos:** Showcasing consumer Web3 onboarding through Telegram Mini Apps and [ton-connect](/resources/glossary/ton-connect).
