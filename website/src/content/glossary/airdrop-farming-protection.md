## Definition: What is Airdrop Farming Protection?

**Airdrop Farming Protection** refers to algorithmic and on-chain verification protocols designed to prevent automated bot scripts, multi-account syndicates, and mercenary bounty hunters from exploiting Web3 event RSVPs strictly to farm potential future token airdrops.

Without anti-farming safeguards, popular crypto conferences and hackathons find their ticket allocations filled within seconds by non-human accounts that never intend to physically attend.

---

## Core Anti-Farming Mechanisms Used by ONTON

1. **Telegram Account Age & Activity Scoring:** Filtering out freshly generated disposable Telegram accounts with zero message history.
2. **Wallet Balance & History Thresholds:** Requiring connecting wallets via [ton-connect](/resources/glossary/ton-connect) to maintain a minimum transaction history or hold established ecosystem Jettons.
3. **Non-Refundable Micro-Deposits:** Requiring a nominal 10-Star ($0.20) commitment fee that makes running 10,000 automated bots economically prohibitive for farmers.
4. **Physical Door Check-In Requirement:** Issuing commemorative [soulbound-token](/resources/glossary/soulbound-token) badges strictly upon scanning a dynamic [qr-code-door-checkin](/resources/glossary/qr-code-door-checkin) at the physical venue door.
