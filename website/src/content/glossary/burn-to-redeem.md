## Definition: What is Burn-to-Redeem?

**Burn-to-Redeem** is an on-chain smart contract mechanic where a digital token, NFT ticket, or voucher is permanently destroyed ("burned") from the user's wallet in exchange for claiming physical merchandise, access to exclusive event areas, or catering vouchers.

By permanently destroying the access token on-chain, organizers ensure that a physical benefit can never be claimed more than once, eliminating counterfeit claims and spreadsheet reconciliation errors.

---

## Common Burn-to-Redeem Use Cases in Web3 Events

* **Exclusive Conference Swag:** Attendees hold a "Hoodie Voucher NFT". At the sponsor booth, scanning their wallet burns the voucher and triggers physical merchandise dispensing.
* **VIP Dinner Entry:** A specialized VIP pass is burned at the dining hall door to guarantee single-use entrance without sharing passcodes.
* **Tier Upgrades:** Attendees burn three general admission passes to mint a single backstage VIP credential.

---

## Advantages for Event Organizers

- **Cryptographic Accounting:** Zero risk of staff giving away more items than authorized by the smart contract.
- **Supply Deflation:** Automatically reduces the circulating supply of event tokens, preserving exclusivity.
- **Seamless Telegram Mini App Flow:** Easily executed inside `@theontonbot` with a single wallet confirmation.
