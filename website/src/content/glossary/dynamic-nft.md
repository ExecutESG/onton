> **Definition:**
> A **Dynamic NFT (dNFT)** is a non-fungible token whose smart contract allows underlying metadata, artwork, or utility attributes to update programmatically in response to external real-world events, oracles, or user actions, rather than remaining static forever.

---

## Dynamic NFTs as Event Passports
In the ONTON ecosystem, an attendee's digital event passport functions as a dynamic credential:
- **Visual Evolution:** The badge visually updates as an attendee completes workshop stages, attends sponsor demos, or checks into afterparties.
- **Tier Upgrades:** Attendees who attend 3 or more ecosystem events automatically unlock VIP organizer privileges and governance voting multipliers.
