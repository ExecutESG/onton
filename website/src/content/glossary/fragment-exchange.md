> **Definition:**
> **Fragment** (`fragment.com`) is the official decentralized platform built on the TON blockchain that facilitates the auction, purchase, and management of Telegram collectible usernames, anonymous virtual numbers, Telegram Premium subscriptions, and the off-ramping of Telegram Stars into TON cryptocurrency.

---

## How Event Organizers Use Fragment
When event organizers sell tickets using [telegram-stars](/resources/glossary/telegram-stars) inside the ONTON Mini App:
- Accumulated Stars can be cashed out directly on Fragment into liquid **TON cryptocurrency**.
- Funds can be reinvested into official Telegram Sponsored Messages to advertise upcoming crypto summits and side events directly to targeted channel audiences.
