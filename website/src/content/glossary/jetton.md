> **Definition:**
> A **Jetton** is the official fungible token smart contract standard (TEP-74) on the TON blockchain, comparable to Ethereum's ERC-20 standard. Jettons represent utility tokens, community currencies, and stablecoins (such as USDT on TON).

---

## Jettons in Event Ticketing
In the ONTON ecosystem, Jettons allow event organizers to:
- **Accept Ecosystem Currencies:** Accept USDT, TON, or native protocol community tokens for conference registrations.
- **Zero-Friction Settlement:** Process payments with block times under 3 seconds and negligible network fees ($0.01).
- **Incentive Distribution:** Distribute sponsor rewards, bounties, and travel grants directly to attendees' non-custodial wallets.
