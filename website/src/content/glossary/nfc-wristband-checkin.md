## Definition: What is NFC Wristband Check-In?

**NFC Wristband Check-In** is the use of Near Field Communication (NFC) RFID chips embedded inside physical wristbands to authenticate attendees, streamline venue entry, and facilitate booth networking at in-person conferences and festivals.

In Web3 events, NFC wristbands are cryptographically paired with an attendee's on-chain wallet or Telegram profile, bridging the gap between physical event access and on-chain attendance verification.

---

## How NFC Check-In Works at Web3 Conferences

1. **Badge Pickup & Binding:** Upon arriving at the venue, the attendee taps their NFC wristband against a door kiosk while scanning their ONTON [qr-code-door-checkin](/resources/glossary/qr-code-door-checkin). This pairs the wristband UID with their on-chain [soulbound-token](/resources/glossary/soulbound-token).
2. **Sub-Second Gate Entry:** For the remainder of the multi-day conference, attendees tap their wristband against turnstiles or handheld staff scanners to enter keynote halls and VIP lounges in under 200 milliseconds.
3. **Tap-to-Connect Networking:** Attendees can tap wristbands with fellow builders or sponsor booths to instantly share Telegram handles or claim digital sponsor swags.

---

## Benefits Over Traditional Paper Badges

- **Eliminates Gate Queues:** High-speed contactless tapping handles thousands of attendees per hour without bottlenecks.
- **Waterproof and Durable:** Ideal for multi-day festivals, hackathon overnight stays, and outdoor side events.
- **Verifiable Proof of Physical Attendance:** Ensures commemorative SBT badges are minted strictly to users who physically stepped through the gate.
