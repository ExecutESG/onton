> **Definition:**
> **Proof of Attendance** is a cryptographic standard where attendees of physical or digital events receive an immutable, timestamped badge verifying their presence. It acts as a digital passport stamp for conferences, hackathons, and community meetups.

---

## Proof of Attendance on the TON Blockchain
ONTON modernizes the legacy POAP model by minting attendance badges natively on the **TON blockchain**:
- **Sub-Second Confirmation:** Door check-in takes less than 2 seconds.
- **Zero Gas for Attendees:** The event organizer covers micro-gas fees or utilizes sponsor credits.
- **Direct Mini App Integration:** Attendees can view, share, and showcase their event passport directly inside their Telegram profile.
