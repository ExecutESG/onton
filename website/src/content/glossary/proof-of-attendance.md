> **Definition:**
> **Proof of Attendance** is a standard where attendees of physical or digital events receive an immutable, timestamped credential verifying their presence. It acts as a digital passport stamp for conferences, hackathons, and community meetups.

---

## Proof of Attendance in ONTON
