> **Definition:**
> **Dynamic QR Door Check-In** is a cryptographic event ticketing verification standard where the visual barcode displayed on an attendee's device continuously regenerates every 30 to 60 seconds based on a time-based one-time password (TOTP) algorithm and wallet signature, rendering static screenshots and shared images invalid.

---

## Security Benefits at Venue Doors
- 🛡️ **Zero Scalping & Screenshot Sharing:** Attendees cannot screenshot a VIP ticket pass and send it to multiple friends.
- ⚡ **Sub-2-Second Verification:** Door staff scan the QR code using the ONTON organizer scanner bot; ticket authenticity is verified on-chain in real time.
- 🎖️ **Instant SBT Minting:** Scanning the QR code simultaneously marks the ticket as redeemed and issues the attendee's permanent [proof-of-attendance](/resources/glossary/proof-of-attendance) credential.
