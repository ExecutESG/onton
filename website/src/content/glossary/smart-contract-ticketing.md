## Definition: What is Smart Contract Ticketing?

**Smart Contract Ticketing** is the issuance, transfer, and validation of event tickets via self-executing decentralized code deployed on a blockchain network. Rather than relying on centralized databases or static PDF QR codes that can be duplicated or screenshotted, smart contract tickets exist as unique digital assets (NFTs or [Soulbound Tokens](/resources/glossary/soulbound-token)) governed by cryptographic rules.

Smart contract ticketing solves the fundamental flaws of legacy ticketing: fraudulent secondary market scalping, unauthorized ticket counterfeiting, and excessive middleman processing fees.

---

## How Smart Contract Ticketing Works

1. **Ticket Minting:** When an organizer creates an event, a smart contract mints ticket tokens with metadata containing event dates, seating tiers, and access credentials.
2. **Programmable Resale Caps:** Smart contracts can enforce strict price ceilings (e.g., maximum resale price equal to face value) or completely prohibit transfers to eliminate secondary scalper bots.
3. **Automated Secondary Royalties:** If an organizer allows ticket resale, the smart contract automatically routes a programmed percentage (e.g., 5% to 15%) of secondary transactions directly back to the event creator's treasury.
4. **Door Check-In Verification:** At the venue door, staff scan a dynamic cryptographic [qr-code-door-checkin](/resources/glossary/qr-code-door-checkin). The smart contract updates the ticket status to `Checked-In`, preventing duplicate entries.

---

## Why Event Organizers Choose Smart Contract Tickets

- **Counterfeit Proof:** Cryptographic signatures prevent fake ticket generation.
- **Zero Scalper Exploitation:** Non-transferable SBT rules ensure only verified community members attend.
- **Perpetual Engagement:** Post-event, the ticket metadata can evolve into a digital collectible or grant discounted access to future conferences.
