> **Definition:**
> **Sybil Resistance** is the security capability of a decentralized network or application to prevent a single malicious actor from controlling multiple pseudo-identities (bots, burner wallets) to subvert voting power, monopolize free ticket allocations, or drain airdrops.

---

## How ONTON Enforces Sybil Resistance for Events
- **Telegram Account Verification:** Cross-references account creation date, Telegram Premium status, and verified phone metadata.
- **Soulbound Credentials:** Requires attendees to possess verifiable prior event SBTs or on-chain reputation before registering for limited-capacity hacker events.
- **Dynamic QR Validation:** Regenerates ticket QR codes every 30 seconds to prevent ticket duplication and scalping.
