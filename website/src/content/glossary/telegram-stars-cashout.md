## Definition: What is Telegram Stars Cashout?

**Telegram Stars Cashout** is the official mechanism enabling developers, bot operators, and event organizers to withdraw earned [telegram-stars](/resources/glossary/telegram-stars) into TON cryptocurrency or reinvest them into subsidized Telegram Ads. Cashouts are processed directly through Telegram's decentralized platform, [Fragment](/resources/glossary/fragment-exchange).

This mechanism allows Web3 conference organizers to accept credit cards from attendees via Apple Pay and Google Play while receiving liquidity in decentralized crypto assets.

---

## The Telegram Stars Cashout Workflow

1. **Accumulation:** Attendees purchase tickets inside `@theontonbot` using Telegram Stars. The Stars accumulate in the organizer's bot balance.
2. **Holding Period:** Telegram requires a standard 21-day security holding period to protect against credit card chargebacks on the app stores.
3. **Fragment Withdrawal:** After the holding window, the organizer connects their TON wallet to `fragment.com/stars` and initiates a withdrawal.
4. **TON Settlement:** The Stars are converted into TON tokens at the current platform exchange rate and deposited into the organizer's non-custodial wallet.

---

## Alternative: Subsidized Telegram Ads Reinvestment

Instead of withdrawing TON, event organizers can convert their earned Stars directly into Telegram Ads credit. Telegram frequently offers up to a **30% bonus subsidy** when reinvesting Stars into platform advertising, creating a cost-effective growth loop to promote future events.
