> **Definition:**
> **Telegram Stars** are the official in-app virtual currency introduced by Telegram to facilitate instant purchases of digital goods, services, and event tickets within Telegram Mini Apps. Users can buy Stars using standard app store payment methods (Apple Pay, Google Pay) or cryptocurrency on Fragment.

---

## Why Telegram Stars Matter for Event Ticketing

1. **Zero Crypto Learning Curve:** Attendees don't need seed phrases or browser extension wallets. They simply tap to buy with their saved Apple Pay or Google Pay account.
2. **Instant Settlement:** Purchases clear in milliseconds within the Telegram Mini App interface.
3. **Developer & Organizer Liquidity:** Earned Stars can be converted directly to TON cryptocurrency via Fragment or used to purchase high-intent Telegram sponsored messages.
