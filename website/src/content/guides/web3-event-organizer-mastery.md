## Phase 1: Event Ideation & Format Selection

Before booking a venue or pitching sponsors, determine the exact archetype of your event:
1. **The Hacker House / BUIDL Hub:** 3-5 days of uninterrupted co-working, mentoring, and demo nights for builders. High retention, high ecosystem impact. Target 50-100 highly technical developers.
2. **The VIP Investor & Founder Dinner:** 25-50 vetted executives. Requires strict vetting, private dining, and [token-gated](/resources/glossary/token-gating) invitations. Emphasize privacy and no-recording rules.
3. **The High-Energy Happy Hour / Mixer:** 150-400 attendees. Sponsored open bar, short lightning talks, and open networking. Best for brand visibility and community onboarding.
4. **The Side-Stage Summit / Workshop:** Half-day or full-day curated panel discussions, technical workshops, and product alpha reveals.

## Phase 2: Budgeting & Web3 Sponsorships

Never fund a side event out of pocket. Web3 protocols, venture funds, layer-1 ecosystems, and developer tooling providers actively allocate $5,000 to $50,000 for high-relevance co-branding:

### Sponsorship Tier Breakdown
- **Tier 1: Title Sponsor ($10,000 - $25,000):** Co-branded banner, opening keynote, dedicated Telegram announcement blast, private VIP booth, and co-host branding on all tickets.
- **Tier 2: Supporting Sponsor ($3,000 - $7,500):** Logo on all promotional materials, panel speaker seat, branded cocktail or coffee bar, and social media shoutouts.
- **Tier 3: Community Partner (Cross-promotion):** Free VIP passes in exchange for promoting the event to their Telegram channel, Discord, or DAO newsletter.

### Sourcing Inbound Sponsors
- **Timing:** Reach out 6 to 8 weeks before the main conference dates. Ecosystem budgets are locked 30 days prior.
- **Pitch Angle:** Quantify attendee caliber. Sponsors care more about having 30 verified protocol CTOs in the room than 500 passive swag collectors.
